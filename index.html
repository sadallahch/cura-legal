Privacy Policy for Cura

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

Cura is an analog camera and community spatial photography app. We collect account information (such as your username and profile details via Firebase Authentication), uploaded weekly public roll cover photos, and EXIF metadata (approximate location area and timestamps) when you publish to the community cluster.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your photos and reflections are stored securely via Firebase Cloud Storage and Firestore to power the weekly community map clusters and Sunday Edition zine. We do not sell or share your personal data with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Security & Deletion

All data transmitted to Firebase is encrypted. You may request complete account and data erasure at any time by contacting our support team.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@curaphoto.app
