Account & Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 2026

If you wish to delete your Cura account and remove all associated data (including profile information, published cluster exposures, and cloud storage files), please follow the steps below:

How to Request Deletion:

  1. Send an email from your registered account email to: support@curaphoto.app
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
  3. Our team will process your request and permanently delete your user profile, authentication credentials, and uploaded media from Firebase within 7 business days.
